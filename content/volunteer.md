          There are many ways you can get involved to help kids with less access to opportunity experience the transformative benefits of sleepaway summer camp. Here are just a few:

          **Transportation Liaison:** greet our campers when they leave for / arrive from camp at the camp bus pickup/dropoff locations around Ann Arbor.

          **Help with Camper Orientation:** volunteer at our yearly Camper Orientation event to help our kids get prepared for camp.

          **Serve on our Board of Directors:** contribute your leadership to help guide Washtenaw Camp Placement now and into the future!

          **Administrative support:** help us with mailings, filing,database management and other administrative tasks.

          **Help With Supplies:** our campers need supplies to bring to camp! Donate needed items, help gather donations, organize a donation drive, etc. Donated items include sleeping bags, sunscreen, bug spray, sheets, flashlights, bath and beach towels, etc. Our <a href="https://www.amazon.com/hz/wishlist/ls/194VTAIUU6UQX?ref_=wl_share" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Amazon Wish List</a> is one way to see the items we need.

          Are you passionate about serving your community? Reach out to us through our <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfLG4ineKUfxBeMRztKBF9hlUDKW5bQX_AkD7EhXltrn06btw/viewform" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volunteer Interest Form</a>.
