There are many ways you can help the youth of your community have a life-changing camp experience.

**Support our program financially!**\
100% of our funding comes from the community, so it is truly thanks to your support that these programs are possible. You can <a href="https://secure.qgiv.com/for/campmatters" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DONATE HERE</a> or mail a check payable to:\
Washtenaw Camp Placement\
3135 S. State St\
Suite 350-D\
Ann Arbor, MI 48108

We also accept direct bank transfers.

**Support our Endowment**\
Help us grow our Charles B. Smith and Peggie J. Hollingsworth Smith Endowment Fund at the Ann Arbor Community Foundation. To give to this fund click <a href="https://aaacf.fcsuite.com/erp/donate/create/fund?funit_id=2584" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a>.

**Donate Needed Gear for Camp**\
Our campers are required to bring a long list of essential items with them to camp. To help reduce the financial burden on families, we spend all year collecting these supplies.

Donations of new or gently used camping gear are greatly appreciated and can be mailed or dropped off at our office at the address above. Items can also be purchased directly from our <a href="https://www.amazon.com/hz/wishlist/ls/194VTAIUU6UQX?ref_=wl_share" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Amazon Wish List</a>. Our Campers need:

Sleeping Bags\
Duffle Bags\
Twin Sheets\
Blankets\
Beach Towels\
Bath Towels\
Wash Cloths\
Soap\
Water Bottles\
Flashlights\
Rain Ponchos\
Sun screen (Non-aerosol)\
Bug repellent (Non-aerosol)\
Cloth Laundry Bags

**Volunteer!**\
There are a number of ways to support Washtenaw Camp Placement with your time and your skills. If you are interested in learning more about volunteer opportunities with us, click [here](/volunteer/).
