# Mission / Values / Vision

::::: columns 1/4 1/2 1/4
:::: column

::: quote
*“I have the conviction that a FEW WEEKS in a well-organized Summer Camp may be of more value educationally than a WHOLE YEAR of formal education.”*

Charles Eliot, who, at the age of 35, was the youngest President of Harvard University.
:::
::::

:::: column
**MISSION**\
Our mission is to ensure that every child, regardless of income or background, has the opportunity to experience the transformative power of summer camp—building resilience, joy, and lifelong potential.

**VALUES**\
**Equity** - camp levels the playing field and should be available to all.\
**Joy** - camp brings joy, and children that are joyful are more willing to learn and to grow.\
**Courage** - to try new things, to discover new things about yourself.\
**Collaboration** - it takes a whole community to support our youth.

**VISION**\
Our vision is of a future where the enormous benefits of camp are not a privilege. Where every child has access to the joy, growth, and self-discovery that summer camp brings. Where they can unplug, explore, and become their best self.
