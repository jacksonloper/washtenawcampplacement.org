          **Privacy Policy for Washtenaw Camp Placement.org:**\
          Washtenaw Camp Placement is committed to protecting your privacy. Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines who we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website.

          We do not share, sell, rent, or distribute your personal information to third parties. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% safe. Additionally, our website may contain links to third party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy policies.

          Your information is used only to:

          -   Review your child’s application for camp.
          -   Respond to your inquiries, comments, or requests
          -   Communicate about camp registration, transportation, packing lists, and important updates
          -   Provide emergency notifications during the summer camp season
          -   Improve and maintain the website and our services

          We may collect the following information:

          -   Personal information – Information that identifies you such as your name, your children’s names, email, address, phone number, and any other details you voluntarily provide through forms on the website.
          -   Usage Data – Information on how you interact with our Website, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the website.
          -   Cookies – We use cookies to enhance your experience on our Website. Cookies are small data files stored on your device. You can control the use of cookies through your browser settings.

          **Terms of Service for SMS Messaging:**\
          By opting in to receive SMS updates from Washtenaw Camp Placement, we collect and store your phone number and name (if provided) solely for the purpose of sending relevant camp-related updates and reminders. You agree to receive periodic text messages related to your child’s camp experience, including reminders, logistical updates, and alerts.

          -   Message and data rates may apply.
          -   Frequency of messages may vary during camp season.
          -   You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
          -   For help, reply HELP or contact us at [afales@washtenawcampplacement.org](mailto:afales@washtenawcampplacement.org)

          Your participation is voluntary. Opting in does not guarantee a camp placement and is only used for communication purposes related to the services we provide.

          **Privacy Policy Updates**\
          Washtenaw Camp Placement reserves the right to update these Terms at any time. Continued use of our messaging service implies acceptance of any updates.
