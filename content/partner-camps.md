          ![Partner Camps](/images/2026/01/partner-camps.png)

          All Washtenaw Camp Placement campers get sent to an amazing YMCA sleepaway summer camp. Our camps are situated all over the state of Michigan.

          <a href="https://www.campagq.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">**Camp AGQ**</a>\
          Situated on beautiful Burt Lake, YMCA Camp AGQ is administered by the Ann Arbor YMCA, yet is our northernmost camp! Learn about our [Heather Sloan Scholarship](/heather-sloan-scholarship).

          <a href="https://hayowentha.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">**Camp Arbutus Hayo-Went-Ha**</a>\
          Camp Arbutus for the gals; both Camp Arbutus and Camp Torch Lake provide a beautiful setting for self-discovery.

          <a href="https://hayowentha.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">**Torch Lake Camp Hayo-Went-Ha**</a>\
          Torch Lake Camp is for the boys; both Camp Arbutus and Torch Lake Camp provide a beautiful setting for self-discovery.

          <a href="https://www.mysticlakecamp.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">**Mystic Lake Camp**</a>\
          The lake that gives the camp its name provides a spectacular backdrop for the transformative experience its campers have.

          <a href="https://ymcadetroit.org/nissokone/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">**Camp Nissokone**</a>\
          On the scenic shores of Lake Huron!

          <a href="https://ymcadetroit.org/ohiyesa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">**Camp Ohiyesa**</a>\
          Our newest partner camp! The sister camp to Nissokone, Camp Ohiyesa is located near Holly, Michigan.

          <a href="https://www.ymcatoledo.org/ymcastorercamps/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">**Storer Camps**</a>\
          Less than an hour’s drive from Ann Arbor, this gem of Southeast Michigan is our closest partner camp.
